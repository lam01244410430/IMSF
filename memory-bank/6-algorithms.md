# Draft algorithms
- Freshness uses days_left = expiry_date minus the household local date. More than 3 days is FRESH, 0 through 3 is WARNING, and negative is EXPIRED. Policy for missing dates awaits review. Generate daily alerts with unique event keys. Compute freshness at read time.
- FEFO orders candidates by expiry, receipt time, and lot ID. Convert to the base unit and allocate by lot. Lock candidates and recalculate before writing. Roll back on shortage. Eligibility and undated lot ordering await review.
- Waste cost is the sum of discarded quantity times lot unit cost within each currency. Aggregate disposal records linked to movements once. The waste-rate denominator awaits review.
- Proposed recipe scoring groups identical ingredients and simulates FEFO using FRESH and WARNING stock. C is the fraction of ingredients with stock. Q is mean quantity fulfillment. E is the mean fraction of demand fulfilled from WARNING lots. P is 0.15 times the fraction of missing ingredients. Clamp 0.45C + 0.25Q + 0.30E - P to [0,1]. Break ties by recipe ID. Any essential shortage makes the recipe unavailable. Substitutes require explicit configuration and shared stock cannot be allocated twice. Scoring details await review.
